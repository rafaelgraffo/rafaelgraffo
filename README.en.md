I built my career in Information Technology across **IT management, team leadership, operations, technical support and infrastructure**. Working daily with systems, people and processes gave me a practical view of service availability, monitoring, operational continuity and continuous improvement.

Building on that foundation, I keep evolving towards **Cloud, DevOps, automation and Software Engineering**, connecting continuous study with hands-on projects while keeping the operational discipline. I value documentation, standardisation, meaningful metrics and reliable solutions.
